Flow

From first deposit to payout in five moves.

  1. 1

    Fund

    Send a single asset into a vault. The vault is tied to one liquidity venue and has no other reach.

  2. 2

    Spread

    Your funds are laid across a band of price steps around the current quote, an equal slice in each.

  3. 3

    Earn

    Whenever a swap walks through your band, your slices collect a cut of what the trader pays.

  4. 4

    Recentre

    If the quote drifts far enough, a keeper slides the band back under it. The cost is itemised.

  5. 5

    Collect

    Claim whenever you like. Fees are split once, at the moment you collect.

Fewer steps packed close to the quote. Each slice sees more traffic, and the band needs recentring more often.

More steps spread across a longer stretch of prices. Quieter earnings per slice, but the quote has further to roam before you move.

Schematic only. Heights are not a forecast.
